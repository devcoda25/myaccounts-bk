## 2024-10-24 - [Missing foreign key indexes on high volume parental control entities]
**Learning:** High-traffic queries on relations such as `findManyByParentId` in ChildProfile, `HouseholdMember` by household, and `ParentalApproval`/`ParentalActivity` by child do not have foreign key indexes automatically generated by Prisma in PostgreSQL. The omission causes full table scans that degrade performance as parental activity logs and approvals grow over time.
**Action:** Manually define `@@index` for critical foreign keys like `parentId` (ChildProfile), `householdId` (HouseholdMember), `childId` (ParentalApproval, ParentalActivity).
