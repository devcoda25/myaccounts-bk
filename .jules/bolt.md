## 2025-02-27 - [Add composite indexes for frequently queried collections]
**Learning:** Found several database queries performing sort operations on non-indexed fields (`createdAt`, `lastUsedAt`, `expiresAt`, `at`) alongside a foreign key filter. Examples include `findUserTickets` sorting by `createdAt: 'desc'`, `findUserReports` sorting by `createdAt: 'desc'`, `findAll` notifications by `createdAt: 'desc'`, `findActiveSessionsByUser` by `lastUsedAt: 'desc'`, `findManyByChildId` and `findPendingByOwner` for parental approvals by `at: 'desc'`, `findManyByChildId` for parental activities by `at: 'desc'`, and `findManyByParentId` for child profiles by `createdAt: 'desc'`. These will cause slow query performance on large datasets.
**Action:** Always verify schema for missing composite indexes on frequently sorted tables and add them to optimize `findMany` queries. Remember to explicitly declare the sort direction (`(sort: Desc)`) to avoid schema drift.
