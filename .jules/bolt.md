## 2025-02-27 - [Optimize Database Queries with Composite Indexes]
**Learning:** The `ParentalActivity` and `ParentalApproval` models are frequently queried by `childId` and sorted by `at` (descending), requiring a composite index `@@index([childId, at(sort: Desc)])` to optimize queries such as `findManyByChildId`. Similarly, the `ChildProfile` model is frequently queried by `parentId` and sorted by `createdAt` (descending), requiring `@@index([parentId, createdAt(sort: Desc)])` for `findManyByParentId`.
**Action:** When adding performance-related composite indexes to `prisma/schema.prisma`, ensure you include a comment explaining the optimization directly above the index (e.g., `// @performance Optimizes findManyByChildId with sorting`). Add explicit sort directions (e.g., `(sort: Desc)`) to match actual query parameters.
