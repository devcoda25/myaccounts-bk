## 2024-05-24 - Missing indexes in high-throughput query patterns
**Learning:** Found several queries performing ORDER BY operations without appropriate composite indexes, especially for `createdAt` and similar timestamp fields combined with a foreign key like `userId` or `childId` or `parentId`. Specifically, `ParentalActivity.findManyByChildId` (filters by `childId`, sorts by `at` desc), `ParentalApproval.findManyByChildId` (filters by `childId`, sorts by `at` desc), `SupportTicket.findUserTickets` (filters by `userId`, sorts by `createdAt` desc), `SecurityReport.findUserReports` (filters by `userId`, sorts by `createdAt` desc), and `Session.findActiveSessionsByUser` (filters by `userId` and `expiresAt`, sorts by `lastUsedAt` desc).

**Action:** Add composite indexes to `prisma/schema.prisma` for these models to avoid in-memory sorting overhead in the database, especially when the number of records per user/child grows. We will implement these one by one. I will pick `ParentalActivity` and `ParentalApproval` composite indexes for `[childId, at]`, and `SupportTicket` and `SecurityReport` composite indexes for `[userId, createdAt]` and `Session` for `[userId, lastUsedAt]`.
