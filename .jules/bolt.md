## 2026-11-02 - [Missing Indexes on Foreign Keys in Parental Control Module]
**Learning:** The `ChildProfile` (on `parentId`), `Household` (on `ownerId`), `HouseholdMember` (on `householdId` and `userId`), `ParentalApproval` (on `childId` and `status`), and `ParentalActivity` (on `childId`) models were missing database indexes for their relation foreign keys and frequently queried fields. This causes full table scans, especially as the number of parental control records grows, leading to an N+1 performance bottleneck when querying children for a parent or approvals/activities for a child.
**Action:** Always verify that frequently queried foreign keys and status fields in Prisma schemas have explicit `@@index` directives, as Prisma (with PostgreSQL) does not automatically index foreign keys.
